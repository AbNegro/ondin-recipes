Fried Rice

Description

This fried rice recipe combines fluffy rice with vegetables, eggs, and savory seasonings. It is a simple and satisfying meal that works well as a main dish or a side. Ready in under 30 minutes, it uses common pantry ingredients and can be customized with proteins like chicken, shrimp, or tofu for a fuller meal.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice (preferably day-old)
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 2 eggs, lightly beaten
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (peas, carrots, corn)
  • 2 green onions, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon sesame oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Heat vegetable oil in a large pan or wok over medium-high heat.
  2. Scramble the eggs until just set, then remove them from the pan.
  3. Add more oil if needed, then stir-fry the mixed vegetables until tender.
  4. Add the cooked rice and stir to combine with the vegetables.
  5. Stir in soy sauce and sesame oil, mixing evenly through the rice.
  6. Return the cooked eggs to the pan and fold them into the rice.
  7. Season with salt and pepper, then garnish with sliced green onions before serving.
