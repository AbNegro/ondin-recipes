Lasagna Recipe

This is the lasagna recipe for the ondin project.

Description

This classic lasagna layers tender sheets of pasta with a rich meat and tomato sauce, creamy béchamel (or ricotta), and a generous topping of melted mozzarella and Parmesan. It's baked until bubbling and golden — comforting and perfect for family meals. Preparation includes simmering the sauce, assembling the layers, and baking until the flavors meld. Leftovers reheat beautifully and often taste even better the next day.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles (or no-boil sheets)
  • 1 lb (450g) ground beef or a mix of beef and pork
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2-3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 28 oz (800g) crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp dried oregano, 1 tsp dried basil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 2 cups ricotta or béchamel sauce
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
  • Olive oil for cooking

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Cook lasagna noodles according to package instructions if using regular noodles; drain and set aside.
  2. In a large skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat. Sauté onion until translucent, add garlic and cook 1 minute more.
  3. Add ground meat and brown well. Stir in tomato paste, crushed tomatoes, oregano, basil, salt, and pepper. Simmer 15–20 minutes to develop flavor.
  4. Spread a thin layer of sauce in a baking dish. Layer noodles, sauce, dollops of ricotta (or béchamel), and a sprinkle of mozzarella. Repeat to build 3–4 layers, finishing with sauce and remaining mozzarella and Parmesan on top.
  5. Cover with foil and bake 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake another 10–15 minutes until cheese is golden and bubbly. Let rest 10 minutes before slicing.
